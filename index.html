Around the ride

The rest of riding, kept in one place.

It asks before it records

Rode off without pressing Ride? AROV notices a ride starting and asks once. It never records on its own.

A garage that counts

Your bikes, fuel per tank and maintenance due by the kilometres you actually rode, with a reminder when a job falls due.

Routes for riders

Plan a route that prefers twisty roads and skips motorways, then follow spoken directions with the phone in its mount.

Ride with a crew

Plan group rides, see where everyone is while you ride together, and see who leads and who sweeps.

Notes on the road

Leave a note where the gravel is or the view is worth stopping for, and read what other riders left.

Your home stays off the map

Shared rides and live positions leave out the first and last 500 m, so nobody sees where you start and finish. AROV asks after each ride whether to share it, and the answer starts at no.
