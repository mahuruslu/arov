Terms of use

The rules for riding together.

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who these are with

AROV is made by Mahur Uslu, an independent developer. By creating an account and using AROV you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app. Questions go to support@arov.app.

Riding comes first

  • Do not handle the phone while riding. Start a ride before you set off, and leave the phone in its mount.
  • AROV is not a safety device. Speed, lean angle, directions, fuel range and road notes are estimates from a phone and from other riders, and can be wrong. Ride by the road in front of you and by the law where you are.
  • You are responsible for how you ride, including in a group ride you joined through AROV.

Your account

You need an account to use AROV. Keep the email address it uses yours: whoever can read it can sign in. You must be at least 13, and old enough where you live to agree to these terms. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

What you share with other riders

You decide what other riders see: rides you share, your profile, photos, posts in crews and the forum, comments and road notes. What you share stays yours. By sharing it you allow AROV to store it and show it to the riders you shared it with, for as long as you keep it shared.

No room for abuse

AROV has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive riders. Do not post or send anything that is:

  • harassing, threatening, hateful or discriminatory;
  • sexual, violent or otherwise objectionable;
  • somebody else's private information, including where they live, or a photo of them without their consent;
  • spam, advertising, or misleading, including false road notes;
  • illegal, or encouraging dangerous or illegal riding.

Every post, photo, comment and note can be reported, and any rider can be blocked. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks these terms is removed, and a rider who posts it can be suspended or removed from AROV without notice.

Using AROV fairly

Do not try to reach other riders' data, overload or break the service, or use AROV in a way that breaks the law.

The service

AROV is provided as it is. It may change, have interruptions, or lose data despite care taken; keep anything you cannot lose elsewhere too. As far as the law allows, AROV is not liable for loss or damage arising from using it, including on the road.

Ending

You can stop using AROV and delete your account whenever you like. AROV can suspend or close an account that breaks these terms.

Changes

If these terms change, the new version is posted here with a new date. Using AROV after that means you accept it.
